Compare feedback mechanisms

Negative feedback

As the regulated variable changes, the hormonal response opposes the original disturbance. As the variable returns towards its normal range, the stimulus for hormone release decreases.

Example: Thyroid hormone inhibits upstream stimulation from the hypothalamus and anterior pituitary.

Positive feedback

The hormonal response reinforces the initiating stimulus, producing further hormone release. The cycle continues until an event removes the stimulus.

Example: Cervical stretch → oxytocin → stronger contractions → more stretch, until delivery.

Key question: does the response oppose the original change or amplify it?
