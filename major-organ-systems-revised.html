Human organ systems11 systems working together to support cells and maintain homeostasis
Control and coordination
Structures

Brain, spinal cord, nerves and sense organs.

Functions

Detects changes inside and outside the body, interprets signals and activates muscles and glands; fast-acting control.

Structures

Hormone-secreting glands.

Functions

Releases hormones into the bloodstream to influence target organs, growth, reproduction and nutrient use; generally slower-acting control.

Support and movement
Structures

Skeletal muscle, cardiac muscle and smooth muscle tissue.

Functions

Moves body parts and fluids through internal organs, maintains posture and produces heat.

Structures

Bones, ligaments and cartilages.

Functions

Provides support and protection, sites for muscle attachment, blood cell production and mineral storage.

Transport and exchange
Structures

Heart, arteries, capillaries, veins and blood.

Functions

Pumps and transports blood carrying respiratory gases, nutrients, hormones and wastes.

Structures

Nasal cavity, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi and lungs.

Functions

Moves air and exchanges gases: oxygen enters blood and carbon dioxide is expelled.

Structures

Mouth, tongue, teeth, salivary glands, pharynx, oesophagus, stomach, liver, gallbladder, pancreas and intestines.

Functions

Breaks down food, absorbs nutrients and eliminates indigestible material as faeces.

Structures

Kidneys, ureters, urinary bladder and urethra.

Functions

Removes wastes, regulates water and electrolyte balance, acid–base balance and blood pressure; stores and excretes urine.

Protection, defence and reproduction
Structures

Skin, hair and nails.

Functions

Protects internal structures, regulates body temperature and synthesises vitamin D.

Structures

Lymphatic vessels, lymph, lymph nodes, thymus and spleen.

Functions

Returns tissue fluid to blood, transports fatty substances from digestive organs and helps defend against pathogens.

Male structures

Testes, scrotum, penis, accessory glands and ducts.

Male functions

Produces and transfers sperm; secretes male sex hormones.

Female structures

Ovaries, uterine tubes, uterus and vagina.

Female functions

Produces eggs and female sex hormones; receives sperm and supports embryo and foetal development.
