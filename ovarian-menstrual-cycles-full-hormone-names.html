Ovarian and menstrual cycles

Explore each cycle independently, then see how events in the ovary and uterus occur together on the same timeline.

Ovarian cycle

Location: ovaries

Follicular phase: ovarian follicles develop and oestrogen increases.

Ovulation: the luteinising hormone surge triggers release of a secondary oocyte.

Luteal phase: the corpus luteum produces progesterone and oestrogen; it degenerates if pregnancy does not occur.

Menstrual cycle

Location: uterus (endometrium)

Menstrual phase: declining ovarian hormones lead to shedding of the functional layer.

Proliferative phase: oestrogen promotes endometrial repair and growth.

Secretory phase: progesterone promotes glandular secretion and preparation for implantation.

The cycles are simultaneous, but their phase names describe events in different organs. Timing shown is an illustrative 28-day cycle.
