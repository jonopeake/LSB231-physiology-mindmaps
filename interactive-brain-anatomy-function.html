Sensory and motor areas

Compare where general somatic sensations are interpreted and where voluntary movement is controlled.

Sensory: body to cortex

General somatic sensory information from the right side of the body is interpreted in the left primary somatic sensory area, in the postcentral gyrus of the parietal lobe.

The sensory pathways are crossed.

Where are these areas?

Primary somatic sensory area: postcentral gyrus, parietal lobe. Primary motor area: precentral gyrus, frontal lobe.

The body is represented upside-down in these cortical maps. The lips and fingertips have relatively large sensory representations; the face, mouth and hands have large motor representations associated with fine control.
